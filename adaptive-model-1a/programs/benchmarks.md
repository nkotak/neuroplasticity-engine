# Benchmarks

Benchmark files:
- `data/benchmarks/research_mini_v1.jsonl`
- `data/benchmarks/selflearn_v1.jsonl`

Task types:
- `code`
- `math`
- `grounded_qa`
- `instruction`

Promotion gate defaults:
- composite score improvement: `+2.0` absolute points
- max bucket regression: `-1.0`
- validation loss worsening tolerance: `0.05`

Benchmark gold sets are protected and must never be edited by autonomous runs.
